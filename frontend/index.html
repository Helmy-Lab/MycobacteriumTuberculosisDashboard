About this study

The MtB Experiment Dashboard brings published Mycobacterium tuberculosis (H37Rv) transcriptomic and proteomic datasets together in one place. Instead of opening each study's supplementary tables separately, look up a gene or protein and see how its expression changes across experiments, then compare those changes between studies.

At the heart of the project is transcriptional plasticity: the ability of M. tuberculosis to reshape which genes it expresses, and how strongly, as its surroundings change. Placing many conditions side by side shows which genes respond consistently and which respond only to particular challenges.

      How to read the data

      Relative datasets report log2 fold changes against a control or reference: positive values mean over-expressed, negative values mean under-expressed.

      Absolute datasets report expression or protein abundance directly, so they show how strongly a gene or protein is present rather than whether it changed.

      Relative expression comparison

      Relative log2FC = Condition A − Condition B (choose A and B from your selected conditions).

      Cells with |log2FC| below the minimum threshold are left blank. Click a cell for gene and publication details. Genes are ordered by Rv number.

      Scatterplot

      Each point is a gene; its X and Y values are the mean of the conditions chosen for each axis. The line is the least-squares linear fit and R² is how much of the variation in Y it explains.

      Only datasets with adjusted P-values are listed.

      Volcano plot

      Over-expressed genes sit on the right, under-expressed on the left. Coloured points have adjusted P < 0.05 and |log2FC| beyond the threshold.
